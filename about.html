About SSUCOEN

Understanding our mission, organizational structure, and enduring commitment to the Nigerian educational sector.

Who We Are

The Senior Staff Union in Colleges of Education, Nigeria (SSUCOEN) is a premier national trade union representing the collective interests of senior non-teaching staff across the Federation.

Guided by our motto, "Labour for Honour and Survival", SSUCOEN serves as a critical pillar within Nigeria's tertiary education ecosystem. We recognize that the effective administration and operational success of Colleges of Education rely heavily on the dedication, expertise, and welfare of senior administrative, technical, and professional staff.

As a democratic and organized labor body, SSUCOEN actively advocates for equitable working conditions, fair remuneration, structural development, and the professional elevation of our members. Beyond staff welfare, the union is deeply invested in the institutional growth of Colleges of Education, partnering with government agencies, college management, and other educational stakeholders to formulate policies that ensure the sustained production of quality teachers for the nation.

Our Vision

"To be a premier, highly responsive, and democratic trade union that ensures a dignified, secure, and equity-driven working environment for senior non-teaching staff, while serving as a cornerstone for world-class teacher education and administrative excellence in Nigeria."

Our Mission

"To protect and advance the socio-economic welfare, labour rights, and professional growth of its members through strategic dialogue, proactive collective bargaining, and active partnership with educational stakeholders, ensuring the stability and development of Colleges of Education across the federation."

The Role of SSUCOEN

Our core objectives translate into actionable responsibilities that shape our day-to-day operations and long-term strategy.

Member Welfare

We engage in continuous negotiation to ensure fair remuneration, prompt payment of entitlements, provision of modern work tools, and comprehensive health and safety standards for all members.

Industrial Relations

Acting as the formal bridge between staff and management, we prioritize industrial harmony through mediation, conflict resolution, and structured dialogue to prevent academic and administrative disruptions.

Professional Advancement

SSUCOEN champions the continuous capacity building of its members, advocating for regular training, equitable promotion processes, and opportunities for academic and administrative upskilling.

Institutional Development

We function as critical stakeholders in the infrastructural and academic growth of Colleges of Education, pushing for adequate government funding and efficient resource utilization.

Policy Engagement

SSUCOEN holds a decisive voice in shaping state and national educational policies. We participate in technical committees and legislative hearings concerning tertiary education in Nigeria.

Unity & Accountability

We uphold strict democratic principles in our governance structure, ensuring transparent leadership and fostering an unbreakable bond of solidarity among our nationwide chapters.

Our History

SSUCOEN emerged out of the historical necessity to provide a distinct, unified platform for the senior non-teaching workforce within Nigeria's Colleges of Education.

Prior to its formation, the specific administrative and technical challenges faced by senior non-academic staff were often subsumed under broader educational unions. Recognizing the specialized nature of administrative functions in teacher-training institutions, visionary leaders united to establish an independent body.

Over the years, SSUCOEN has grown into a formidable national entity. We have successfully negotiated numerous collective agreements, stabilized the administrative frameworks of new and existing colleges, and championed legislative reforms that have directly improved the lives of thousands of institutional workers.

Our Core Values

  • Solidarity

    United in purpose across all chapters, recognizing that an injury to one is an injury to all.

  • Integrity

    Upholding the highest standards of transparency, accountability, and ethical conduct in all union affairs.

  • Professionalism

    Commitment to administrative excellence and the continuous improvement of the educational sector.

  • Equity

    Ensuring fairness, justice, and equal opportunity for every member regardless of gender or geography.
