About SSUCOEN

The bedrock of administrative excellence and welfare protection in Nigeria's Colleges of Education.

Who We Are

Advocates for the Dignity of Labour in Education

The Senior Staff Union in Colleges of Education, Nigeria (SSUCOEN) is a legally recognized, democratic trade union formed to represent the collective interests of all senior non-teaching staff across Federal, State, and privately-owned Colleges of Education in the country.

Operating with the motto "Labour for Honour and Survival," SSUCOEN stands as a formidable pillar in the Nigerian educational sector. We believe that the quality of teacher education is deeply intertwined with the welfare, morale, and professional capacity of the administrative and technical staff who manage these critical institutions.

Our History

Decades of Struggle and Progress

SSUCOEN was established out of a critical necessity to give a unified, powerful voice to the senior administrative, professional, and technical staff in Colleges of Education. Before its inception, there was a significant gap in the robust representation of non-teaching personnel, leading to disparities in welfare and career progression compared to their counterparts in other tertiary institutions.

Over the years, the union has grown from a loose coalition of localized associations into a highly organized national body. Through strategic dialogue, principled negotiations, and unyielding solidarity, SSUCOEN has successfully campaigned for standardized schemes of service, improved funding for colleges, and enhanced remuneration packages for its members nationwide.

Our Vision

"To be a premier, highly responsive, and democratic trade union that ensures a dignified, secure, and equity-driven working environment for senior non-teaching staff, while serving as a cornerstone for world-class teacher education and administrative excellence in Nigeria."

Our Mission

"To protect and advance the socio-economic welfare, labour rights, and professional growth of its members through strategic dialogue, proactive collective bargaining, and active partnership with educational stakeholders, ensuring the stability and development of Colleges of Education across the federation."

What We Do

Core Objectives

The strategic goals that drive our union's actions and engagements.

Member Welfare & Remuneration

To negotiate and secure appropriate salaries, allowances, pensions, and comprehensive welfare packages that reflect current economic realities and the value of our members' contributions.

Professional Advancement

To champion capacity building, sponsor training programs, and ensure clear, unhindered career progression pathways for all administrative and technical staff within the system.

Industrial Harmony

To promote a peaceful, productive working environment by resolving disputes through constructive dialogue, collective bargaining, and lawful arbitration.

Institutional Funding

To actively lobby government and relevant agencies for adequate funding, infrastructural development, and technological upgrade of all Colleges of Education in Nigeria.

Policy & Governance Engagement

To participate actively in policy formulation and governance structures that affect teacher education and the management of tertiary institutions nationally.

Unity & Accountability

To foster a spirit of solidarity among members, ensuring transparent leadership, democratic practices, and strict accountability in the administration of the union.

Our Commitment to Members

SSUCOEN operates on the principle that an empowered workforce is the engine of institutional success. We are unwaveringly committed to ensuring that no member suffers victimization, unjust termination, or marginalization in the workplace.

Protection of Rights

Defending members against unfair labor practices and providing legal support when institutional rights are breached.

Open Communication

Maintaining transparent channels of communication between the national leadership, state chapters, and individual members.

Strategic Partnerships

Collaborating with the NCCE (National Commission for Colleges of Education), Federal Ministry of Education, and other sister unions (COEASU, NASU) to drive holistic sector development.
