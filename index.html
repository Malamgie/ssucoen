Who We Are

A Strong Voice for Administrative Excellence in Teacher Education.

The Senior Staff Union in Colleges of Education, Nigeria (SSUCOEN) is the umbrella body representing the collective interests of all senior non-teaching staff in Federal, State, and private Colleges of Education across the federation.

We are committed to fostering industrial harmony, protecting labour rights, and ensuring that our members have a dignified and secure working environment, thereby contributing to the production of world-class teachers in Nigeria.

  • Advocating for equitable remuneration and welfare.
  • Promoting professional capacity building and career growth.
  • Partnering with educational stakeholders for institutional stability.

Our Vision

"To be a premier, highly responsive, and democratic trade union that ensures a dignified, secure, and equity-driven working environment for senior non-teaching staff, while serving as a cornerstone for world-class teacher education and administrative excellence in Nigeria."

Our Mission

"To protect and advance the socio-economic welfare, labour rights, and professional growth of its members through strategic dialogue, proactive collective bargaining, and active partnership with educational stakeholders."
