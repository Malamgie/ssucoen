Who We Are

The Voice of Senior Non-Teaching Staff in Nigeria.

SSUCOEN is a formidable, democratic trade union dedicated to the socio-economic welfare, labour rights, and professional growth of senior non-teaching staff in Colleges of Education nationwide.

Through proactive collective bargaining, strategic dialogue, and partnership with educational stakeholders, we ensure stability and administrative excellence within the Nigerian educational sector.

Read Our Full History

Our Vision

"To be a premier, highly responsive, and democratic trade union that ensures a dignified, secure, and equity-driven working environment for senior non-teaching staff, while serving as a cornerstone for world-class teacher education and administrative excellence in Nigeria."

Our Mission

"To protect and advance the socio-economic welfare, labour rights, and professional growth of its members through strategic dialogue, proactive collective bargaining, and active partnership with educational stakeholders..."

Core Objectives

Guiding principles that drive our mandate and operations across the federation.

Member Welfare

Negotiating equitable remuneration and ensuring dignified working conditions for all members.

Professional Advancement

Fostering capacity building and continuous professional development for administrative excellence.

Industrial Harmony

Promoting peaceful dispute resolution and fostering constructive employer-employee relations.

Institutional Infrastructure

Advocating for adequate funding and development of Colleges of Education in Nigeria.

Policy Engagement

Actively participating in formulating educational and labour policies at state and federal levels.

Unity & Accountability

Maintaining a democratic, transparent, and united front across all chapters and zones.

Our Reach

National Presence

SSUCOEN operates through a highly organized structure spanning the National Executive Council, six geopolitical zones, and local chapters in institutions across the 36 states and the FCT.
