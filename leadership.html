The Administrative Engine

The National Secretariat, located in Abuja, serves as the permanent administrative headquarters of SSUCOEN. It ensures the seamless execution of the Union's mandate, coordinating between the National Executive Council and the various State Chapters.

Staffed by dedicated administrative professionals, the Secretariat handles member inquiries, processes grievances, manages official documentation, and organizes the logistical frameworks for national summits and delegates' conferences.

Standing Committees

Specialized bodies constituted to handle specific operational, welfare, and disciplinary matters on behalf of the National Executive Council.

Disciplinary Committee

Ensures adherence to the Union's code of conduct and investigates allegations of constitutional breaches by members or officers.

Welfare & Benefits Committee

Formulates policies and reviews frameworks regarding member remuneration, health insurance, and retirement benefits.

Education & Training Committee

Develops capacity-building programs, workshops, and recommends educational sponsorships for the professional growth of administrative staff.
