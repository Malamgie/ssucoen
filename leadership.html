Institutional Representation

Local Chapter Executives

SSUCOEN's strength lies in its grassroots organization. Every recognized College of Education maintains a democratically elected Local Chapter Executive Council.

These executives serve as the primary interface between members and college management, ensuring workplace harmony and rapid resolution of localized administrative issues.
