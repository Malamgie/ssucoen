Organizational Structure

National Executive Council (NEC)

The highest administrative and executive body of the union, seated in Abuja.

Six Geopolitical Zones

Coordinating union activities across North-Central, North-East, North-West, South-East, South-South, and South-West regions.

Local Chapters

The foundational base of the union operating within individual Colleges of Education nationwide.

Our Members

National Executive Council

The NEC is responsible for driving national policy, conducting strategic negotiations, and ensuring unity across the federation.

Zonal Leadership

Decentralized administration ensuring effective representation across Nigeria's six geopolitical zones.

Institutional Representation

Local Chapter Executives

SSUCOEN's strength lies in its grassroots organization. Every recognized College of Education maintains a democratically elected Local Chapter Executive Council.

These executives serve as the primary interface between members and college management, ensuring workplace harmony and rapid resolution of localized administrative issues.
